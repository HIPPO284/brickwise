How it works

From a messy pile to your next build.

The first version focuses on one practical job: turning loose pieces into a useful inventory.

01

Take a photo

Spread your pieces on a table and photograph them with your phone.

02

Review the scan

AI detects piece type, dimensions, and color. You can correct uncertain matches.

03

Find something to build

Match your inventory with compatible projects and see exactly what is missing.
