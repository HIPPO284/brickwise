How beta validation data is handled

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Brickwise AI is an independent early-stage product concept. This notice covers information submitted through the beta waitlist and product-research forms.

Information collected

We may collect your email address, selected collection size, stated problem, written product feedback, submission time, and a shortened one-way hash derived from your network address for basic abuse prevention.

How it is used

The information is used to operate the beta waitlist, contact invited testers, understand user needs, prioritize product features, prevent spam, and assess whether the product should be developed.

Storage and sharing

Submissions are stored in the project database. Data is not sold. It may be processed by infrastructure providers used to host the website. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the feedback field.

Retention and deletion

Validation data may be retained while the beta is active and for a reasonable period afterward. Before public launch, replace this placeholder with a working contact address through which users can request access or deletion.

Children

This validation page is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children below the minimum age permitted in their jurisdiction. A production version should include an age-appropriate consent design.

Brand independence

Brickwise AI is not affiliated with or endorsed by LEGO, BrickLink, or any other toy manufacturer or marketplace. Third-party names belong to their respective owners.

Contact

Before publishing the site, replace this paragraph with the operator’s business contact email and legal identity where required.

Technical analytics

Brickwise records page views, referral source, campaign tags, and one-way shortened hashes derived from technical request data to estimate daily unique visitors. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this beta site.
